## Patterns

1. **A fix round decides what nobody decided.** Fix rounds closed a finding by choosing a behavior or guessing a
   fact the sandbox can't check, and the next round reopened it:
   - #330 round 2 M-2, a repeat of M-4: the fix refused helper paths with spaces instead of quoting them, because
     how `sbx` quotes couldn't be checked in the sandbox.
   - #330 round 2 M-1, a repeat of M-3: the cleanup warning went to `task.json` only.
   - #314 round 2 M-1, a repeat of round 1's: fixed for `run`, not for `run show`, the same kind.

   3 repeats in 2 tasks. The fix-the-kind rule (decision 188) covers the third; the first two needed an outside fact
   or a choice.
2. **External tools changed under a spike's facts, and the real check came last.**
   - #330: `agy` updated from S16's version to 1.4.12 within 6 days. Its token file moved under `token`, and no secret
     in its binary refreshes the login any more. Found only by the real sign-in, after 4 review rounds and about
     6 hours.
   - #330's cloud run: `sbxm-dev`'s install step ran in 24 s in S14 (decision 254), but now exits 100 at cloud
     create (G67, #337).
   - Milestone 4's end-to-end check found G67-G70 after seven review rounds.
3. **Review rounds continue after the first round with 0 must-fix.**
   - #322: rounds 4, 5 and 6 each found one new docs should-fix, fixed by hand, then another round.
   - #329: rounds 2 and 3 after a round 1 with 0 must-fix.

   5 rounds after a 0-must-fix round, each a few minutes of review plus a hand fix. `sdlc-code-review` already
   said to stop there and file the rest; the session didn't follow it.
4. **C: ran out during tasks** (not a process change; a code issue). Every case was a local worker sandbox left
   running after its commits were saved:
   - #287: review refused at 2.8 GiB.
   - #314: C: filled and the sandbox went read-only; later a resume was refused.
   - #338: review refused at 2.6 GiB while #330's worker sandbox stood.
   - Milestone 4: about 19 GiB per worker, so `--ignore-disk` for every review.

   The rule "remove the worker sandbox once the commits are saved" is done by hand (memory), and each miss costs a
   refusal or a restart.
5. **The user's own steps.** `!` commands and terminal steps in the window:
   - `sbx setup` three times and `sbx secret rm` twice (two logins went wrong: an expired template, G64, and an API
     key, #330).

## Proposals

At most three, ranked by the time they would save.

### 1. A fix round doesn't choose; it stops with "Needs a decision"

- **Problem:** pattern 1. 3 repeat findings in 2 tasks (#330 twice, #314 once). #330's cost one extra round and a
  38-minute stop.
- **Change** (the fix-round prompts `prompts/fix.md` and `prompts/fix-spec.md`): "If fixing a
  finding needs a fact you can't check from inside the sandbox (how an external tool behaves), or a choice between
  behaviors that the issue, the decisions or the review don't settle, don't pick one. Leave that finding unfixed and
  write it under `## Needs a decision` in `result.md`: what you'd need to know or choose, and the options."
- **Measure:** repeat findings per task (this window: 3 in 2 of the 10 recorded tasks).
- **Trial:** the next three tasks with a fix round.
- **Cost:** a stop instead of a guess, so the user may be asked sooner. It adds no step when there's nothing to
  decide.

### 2. Check external facts first and run the real check early

- **Problem:** pattern 2. A self-updating tool (`agy`) and the cloud image changed within days of their spikes (#330,
  #337). The real check ran last, after 4 rounds and 6 hours on #330.
- **Change** (`AGENTS.md`'s no-open-questions rule, decision 244's question set): "Before a worker starts on an issue
  that depends on an external tool's behavior a spike recorded, re-run the spike's key check by hand. After the
  worker, before the review rounds, run the issue's real test (ignored test or by-hand smoke) and hand the result to
  the review."
- **Measure:** problems found by real use after the review rounds ended (this window: 2 on #330; G67-G70 in milestone
  4's end-to-end check).
- **Trial:** the next three issues that depend on an external tool.
- **Cost:** 5-20 minutes per such issue, sometimes with the user (logins). It adds process, but only for issues that
  name an external tool.

### 3. Stop the review loop at the first round with 0 must-fix

- **Problem:** pattern 3. 5 rounds after a 0-must-fix round (#322 three, #329 two).
- **Change** (`sdlc-code-review` and decision 251's re-review): "After a round with 0 must-fix, fix its should-fix
  items by hand. Re-review only that fix's diff (narrow). File anything the narrow re-review finds outside that diff
  as an issue with a trigger, not as another round."
- **Measure:** review rounds after the first 0-must-fix round (this window: 5).
- **Trial:** the next three PRs that reach a 0-must-fix round with should-fix items.
- **Cost:** some should-fix items wait as issues. It removes process.

### Code issue (not an experiment)

- **Local tasks remove the worker sandbox once its commits are saved** (pattern 4), as the cloud already does
  (`stop_cloud_worker`); `task resume` rebuilds it. Size M: the local release in `task::pipeline`, `resume`'s path
  and their fake-backend tests.
