### User rules made in this window

Kept as working rules since: short messages and one question at a time; flag over- and under-specifying; a PR for
every change; warn on complexity; process changes as experiments (2026-10-01); progress as X of Y and at most 3
sessions; a known binary for long jobs; workers install nothing; optimize just in time; notify when long jobs end.
