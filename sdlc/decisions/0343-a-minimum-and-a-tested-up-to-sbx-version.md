343. **A minimum and a tested-up-to `sbx` version (2026-11-22, the user's choice, planning round 1 of milestone 6):** `sbx` releases about weekly, and this upgrade was hard because eight releases piled up. sbxm requires at least 0.54.0 [334] and records the newest version it was tested with; `sbxm doctor` warns, without refusing, when the installed `sbx` is newer. Each later upgrade is a small step: re-run S19's checklist on the new version, compare the captured output, and raise the tested-up-to version. A minimum only was the other option, not chosen: nothing would say when an untested `sbx` is installed.
