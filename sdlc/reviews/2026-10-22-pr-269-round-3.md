**Where:** `src/run/results.rs:106`, `src/run/kits.rs:31`
**What happens:** each `run.json` harness entry records its profile, config hash and kit paths but not `HarnessKits.template`. Rebuilding a template creates a fresh tag and may freeze a different agent version, yet two otherwise identical runs have indistinguishable recorded image provenance.
